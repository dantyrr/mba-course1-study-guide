# MBA Course 1 Study Guide

## &#9658; Open the study guide: **https://dantyrr.github.io/mba-course1-study-guide/**

Interactive study site for Course 1: chapter notes, flashcards, practice quizzes, formula sheets, and worked calculation problems.

- **Economics** — Principles of Economics (Mankiw 10e): Chapters 1, 2, 4, 5, 15
- **Accounting** — Accounting (Warren 29e): Chapters 1–4
- **Finance** — Corporate Finance (Ehrhardt & Brigham 8e) slides: Chapters 2–4
- **Finance Book** — Corporate Finance textbook chapters: Ch 2–4 (course Ch 13–15)
- **Statistics** — Statistics for Business and Economics (Anderson et al. 15e): Ch 2, 3, 4, 7, 9, 10 (course Ch 19–24)

Original study content: notes, flashcards, chapter quizzes, per-chapter practice question banks, randomized practice exams, printable formula sheets (Economics, Finance, Finance Book, Statistics), and Problem Solver pages with every calculation problem worked step by step. Progress — notes read, flashcards mastered, best quiz scores — is saved privately in your browser via localStorage.

Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. To run locally, open `index.html` or serve the folder.
